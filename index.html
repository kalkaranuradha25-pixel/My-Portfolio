01 — Who I Am

About Me

I'm Anuradha — a recently graduated Information Technology engineer from MKSSS's Cummins College of Engineering for Women, Pune. My curiosity sits at the intersection of data, algorithms, and impact — turning sparse, high-volume data into models that change outcomes. I'm equally drawn to building from the ground up: experimenting with unfamiliar problems and turning promising ideas into things that actually exist.

That curiosity took shape during my time as a Data Science Intern on Cummins India's Global AI and Analytics team, where I worked on machine learning prognostics for engine assets and production automation. Alongside the technical work, I've taken on leadership roles across my college — from managing a six-figure student budget to running outreach that encourages more girls into technology. I'm now looking for opportunities to keep building at the intersection of AI/ML research, data science, and real-world problem solving.
